Key Takeaways — CIA Triad Across All Phases

Confidentiality

At risk from the very first phase. The moment an attacker successfully performs recon, private information has already been compromised. Phases 1, 2, 3, and 4 all threaten confidentiality.

Integrity

The most underestimated threat. Integrity is attacked in Phase 3 (data manipulation), Phase 4 (system file modification), and Phase 5 (log tampering). If logs are gone, investigations fail.

Availability

Often overlooked until it's too late. Even passive scanning (Phase 2) can degrade availability. By Phase 4, the attacker controls whether your systems stay online at all.
