# Registered Apprenticeship Program

#### Launch Your Cybersecurity Career with MCCoE

The Missouri Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (MCCoE) offers a 12-month, 2,000-hour Registered Apprenticeship Program designed to develop job-ready cybersecurity professionals. Through hands-on, project-based learning and mentor-guided experiences, apprentices gain practical skills in security operations, risk management, policy development, compliance, and offensive security.​

Cyber Security Support Technician

Program Overview

Brief Overview

- Duration: 12 months (2,000 hours)

- Format: Full-time, hands-on, mentor-guided learning

- Goal: Produce job-ready cybersecurity professionals with practical experience​

Phase 1: Foundation & Orientation

- Onboarding & Orientation: Understand organizational policies, expectations, and professional standards.

- Security Fundamentals & Policy Development: Learn core security principles, develop security policies and procedures, and understand protection strategies for systems and data.​

Phase 2: Defensive Operations

- SOC Analyst Operations: Configure access controls across OS platforms, work with security tools (firewalls, antivirus, EDR, SIEM), monitor network traffic, and respond to security violations.

- Risk Assessment & Compliance: Perform risk assessments, learn GRC frameworks (HIPAA, FERPA, SOC 2, CMMC), and develop risk mitigation strategies.

- Security Collaboration & Issue Resolution: Engage with users to assess access needs, address policy violations, and drive resolution processes.​

Phase 3: Offensive Security

- Penetration Testing: Conduct internal and external penetration tests on networks and web apps, and present executive-level test reports.

- Social Engineering Exercises: Plan and execute phishing campaigns and other social engineering simulations, analyzing behavioral vulnerabilities and user response patterns.​

Phase 4: Awareness, Leadership & Research

- Security Awareness Training: Develop and deliver user security training, promoting best practices and organizational security culture.

- Project Leadership: Lead an end-to-end security project, managing stakeholders, timelines, and deliverables.

- Independent Research Project: Conduct research on a cybersecurity topic of personal or organizational relevance, presenting findings in a professional report or presentation.​

Phase 5: Professional Development & Capstone

- Industry Trends & Career Readiness: Track emerging threats, tools, and strategies in cybersecurity, and prepare for certifications or job transitions.

- Capstone Review & Presentation: Synthesize and present major work from the apprenticeship, with a final evaluation of technical, analytical, and professional competencies.

Program Benefits

### Hands-On Experience

Work in MCCoE's Security Operations Center (SOC), monitoring client networks and responding to threats in real time.

### Mentorship

Receive guidance from experienced cybersecurity professionals throughout the program.

### Job Placement

MCCoE partners with regional employers to place apprentices in cybersecurity roles upon program completion.​
