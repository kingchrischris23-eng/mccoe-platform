# Fostering Collaboration in the Cybersecurity Community

Connecting Minds Creating Resilience

#### Driving Innovation through Our Research

#### Strengthening Local and State Collaboration

MCCoE’s research provides actionable data, in-depth analysis, and strategic recommendations to policymakers, educational institutions, businesses, and key nonprofit organizations. By delivering insights that drive informed decision-making, we help strengthen cybersecurity initiatives, support workforce development, and fuel economic growth– ensuring a more secure digital future for our community.

#### Connecting Industries through Forums

Our dynamic forums create an open dialog between cybersecurity professionals, innovators, educators, and government leaders. These discussions enable problem-solving for industry challenges and quick mobilization around emerging opportunities. By bridging the gap between sectors, we help accelerate the growth of Springfield’s cybersecurity community.

We cultivate partnerships across the region by uniting Missouri’s robust network of nonprofit organizations dedicated to supporting startups, innovation, education, research, and public policy. By connecting these efforts with the state’s professional cybersecurity community, we create opportunities for knowledge-sharing, resource alignment, and coordinated action.

#### Partner with Us to Build a More Secure Future

#### From the Classroom to the Front Lines of Cybersecurity

In 2022, our organization played a key role in a collaborative cybersecurity exercise involving the City of Springfield. As part of a controlled learning experience, a Dury student attempted to hack the city’s systems under the supervision of MCCoE professionals through a network penetration test .

This partnership provided field training while helping secure local cybersecurity defenses. Our goal is to continue facilitating hands-on experiences that bridge education and industry for stronger security. Contact us with ideas or opportunities to develop professionals through live simulations.
