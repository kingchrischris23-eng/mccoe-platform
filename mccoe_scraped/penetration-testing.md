# Network Penetration Testing

Expose Weaknesses Strengthen Defenses

#### How Penetration Testing Enhances Cybersecurity

Our penetration testing (or “pen testing”) services help you identify vulnerabilities in your network before attackers do. By simulating real-world cyber threats, MCCoE identifies security gaps that could be used to compromise your organization. Our expert testing verifies your existing security procedures are effective, and maintain compliance with industry and regulatory standards.

Download Our Penetration Testing Info Sheet

Protecting Your Business with Passion and Precision

Our rigorous penetration testing services provide a comprehensive approach to identifying vulnerabilities and strengthening your security. This process creates security through::

- Simulating attacks in a controlled environment

- Uncovering vulnerabilities in your network, applications, and devices

- Identifying potential threats and breakpoints

- Providing expert analysis and recommendations on how to strengthen defenses

- Achieving compliance and keeping you aligned with best practices

Get Started with Penetration Testing

To ensure a smooth and secure testing process, fill out the Penetration Testing Scope form and complete the Penetration Test Authorization Letter linked below. These documents grant MCCoE consent to test your defenses and define the parameters for this sensitive assessment. By sharing details about your security needs, we match the test to your organization's unique risks and compliance requirements.
