#### Leading the Charge in Cyber Defense

Missouri Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (MCCoE) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to strengthening Southwest Missouri’s regional cybersecurity workforce. We serve university students, early cybersecurity practitioners, and small to medium-sized organizations by providing training and support.

Through access to advanced tools, professional experts, and real-world security experience, our students help create a safer, more knowledgeable community in data and information protection.

#### Why We’re Committed to Building a Safer Digital Future

Information security is evermore at risk due to the lack of technically trained individuals in cybersecurity. As the demand for skilled professionals grows faster than the supply, critical gaps in safety procedures and workforce readiness put organizations and communities vulnerable to attacks. We’re committed to bridging that gap by equipping Southwest Missouri with the knowledge and hands-on skills needed to excel in this field.

By preparing the next generation of cybersecurity experts, we’re not just building careers, we’re strengthening security at the community, regional, state, and national levels. It is our privilege to help expand job opportunities, enhance education, and reinforce cybersecurity resilience for all our partners and the communities they serve.
