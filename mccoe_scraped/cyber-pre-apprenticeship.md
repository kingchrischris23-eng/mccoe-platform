# Registered Apprenticeship Program

#### Launch Your Cybersecurity Career with MCCoE

The Missouri Cybersecurity Center of Excellence (MCCoE) offers an 8-week, 2,00-hour Pre-Apprenticeship Program designed to transition into our full Cyber Security Support Technician Registered Apprenticeship program. Through hands-on, project-based learning and mentor-guided experiences, pre-apprentices gain the knowledge and skills they need to qualify.​

Program Overview

Over the course of our 8-week, 200 hour Pre-Apprenticeship, you will gain experience and knowledge necessary to enter our full Registered Apprenticeship Program.

Program Benefits

### Hands-On Experience

Work in MCCoE's Security Operations Center (SOC), monitoring client networks and responding to threats in real time.

### Mentorship

Receive guidance from experienced cybersecurity professionals throughout the program.

### Job Placement

MCCoE partners with regional employers to place apprentices in cybersecurity roles upon program completion.​
