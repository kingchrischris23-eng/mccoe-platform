Cybersecurity Trainings and Certifications

#### Our Training Programs

#### No-Cost Certification

Our passion is preparing the next generation of network security professionals. We accept students currently enrolled in College IT programs, as well as other qualified individuals looking to begin a career in the field of cybersecurity. Current college students may be eligible to receive college credits and paid internships for being in the program.

#### Career Readiness

Our instructors deliver top-tier training in cybersecurity fundamentals, backed by a facility designed to prepare you for the latest challenges in the field.    You’ll leave our program job-ready and prepared to launch your career in cybersecurity with confidence.

We’re dedicated to expanding access to meaningful information security careers. Upon program completion, every student is eligible to take an industry-recognized certification exam at no cost, helping them take the first step toward meeting the growing demand for cybersecurity professionals.

Hands-On Professional Experience

In addition to training, you will receive practical field experience in our Security Operations Center (SOC).  Here, you will monitor client networks and protect them from threats in real time as a network security analyst.

Students who demonstrate their aptitude may receive opportunities to be involved in more advanced strategic support, such as penetration testing and risk assessment projects with clients.

Committed to Post-Program Job Placement

We partner with employers across the region to place students in job opportunities immediately upon program completion. We care deeply about success beyond the classroom to help them step into meaningful roles that protect our digital infrastructure and strengthen the communities they serve.
