# Network Security Monitoring

Your Networks and Data Our Monitoring and Expertise

Why Your Organization Needs Security Monitoring

As organizations grow, so does the complexity and cost of securing their networks and systems. Hiring internal staff dedicated to monitoring and securing those systems is expensive and requires constant reinvestment in training and IT resources.

For many businesses, outsourcing cybersecurity monitoring services provides better protection at a fraction of the cost. It also ensures that your networks are guarded against threats 24/7/365 instead of just 8 to 5. MCCoE’s Security Operations Center (SOC) provides complete protection with a team of professionals armed with the latest techniques and cutting-edge technology. We’ll handle the complex cybersecurity challenges so you can focus on what you do best.

#### Continuous Monitoring for Complete Peace of Mind

Our experts are on the clock when you’re not, providing vigilance and protection. While your team rests, we’re hard at work safeguarding your organization from cyber threats, ensuring you’re secure– day or night. At the SOC, we provide:

- 24/7 monitoring of your network for cybersecurity threats

- Expert analysis of cybersecurity alerts– and escalation where needed

- Early detection and response to threat alerts and incidents

- Ongoing risk mitigation efforts that address key compliance issues

#### Adapt Your Approach with Strategic Cybersecurity Improvements

Our cybersecurity professionals gather insights from monitoring procedures to help you understand your security posture and provide a roadmap to strengthen your business’s vulnerabilities. Our team will:

- Provide collected data so you can analyze weak points and enhance your defenses

- \Measure improvements over time to ensure continued progress

- Reduce the risk of a successful cyberattack through daily monitoring

We recommend you couple this service with our cybersecurity assessments to get the most actionable understanding of your data.
