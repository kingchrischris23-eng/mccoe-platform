#### Cyberattacks are Inevitable. Is Your Business Ready?

Cybersecurity is no longer optional– it’s essential. Every business and organization is a target as cybercriminals constantly scan for vulnerabilities to exploit.

#### No Industry is Off Limits

#### Your Organization is at Risk

Small to mid-sized organizations are prime targets for cybercriminals– accounting for nearly 60% of all cyberattacks. Hackers know these enterprises often lack robust security, making them easier to exploit.

A cyberattack isn’t just a setback– it can be the end of your business. Studies show that 60% of growing companies shut down within 6 months of a cyber breach. Take action now to protect your business’s future.

#### Cyber Threats Never Stop

#### The High Price of Cyberattacks

Cybercriminals never rest and neither should your defenses. The U.S. is the most targeted country in the world, with over 2,200 cyber attacks happening every day and averaging an attack every 39 seconds.

Cyber attacks come with a hefty price tag. The average cost of a data breach for U.S. companies is a staggering $9.44 million per incident from lost revenue, legal fees, and the cost of recovery.

#### Breaches Suspend Operations

A ransomware attack can stop your organization in its tracks. In less than 4 days, a successful breach can bring all operations to a halt, locking you out of critical systems and data, and creating devastating disruptions for operations.

#### Cyberattacks are Unexpected

Cyberattacks don’t happen on a schedule– they strike when you least expect it. Hackers take advantage of times when your defenses are down, with 27% of attacks occurring over the weekend and 49% after regular weekday hours.
